Do end-to-end content management on your Craft site from whatever AI client you already use. Craft Content MCP adds a [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) server to your site, so Claude, Cursor, Codex, or any other MCP client can read and edit your content directly — at your direction in a chat, or as a step in an agentic workflow or pipeline.

It cuts the time it takes to do real content work. For example:

- Spin up a new page and publish it without touching the Control Panel
- Find and update links across your whole site after a redirect or rebrand
- Rewrite CTAs to match a strategy change
- Swap out images that are no longer brand-aligned
- Build all 8 of your ABM landing pages in one pass
- Add 17 products to your catalog at once

It fits how different teams work:

- **Ecommerce** — bulk-update product descriptions, refresh seasonal collections, and fix pricing copy across hundreds of items
- **Marketing & demand gen** — launch campaign landing pages, update CTAs and offers, and run A/B copy variants
- **Agencies** — onboard a new client site, migrate content in bulk, and apply brand or tone changes across every page
- **Publishers & media** — draft and schedule articles, tag and categorize archives, and update author bios at scale
- **SaaS & docs** — keep changelogs, feature pages, and help content in sync with each release
- **Nonprofits** — refresh program pages, update event details, and keep impact stats current across the site

## How it works

You connect once through your normal Craft login. The assistant gets exactly your permissions — if you can't edit a section in the Control Panel, neither can it. No API keys or service accounts to manage.

New entries start as drafts, and publishing or deleting always asks first. The plugin sticks to content work — no database queries, user management, or system access. A built-in tokens manager and audit log show every connected client and every action taken.

## What's included

15 content tools covering entries, assets, sections, categories, and globals — search and read, create drafts, update fields, publish, soft-delete, and upload assets.

## Requirements

Craft **5.0+**, PHP **8.2+**, and an HTTPS site. Install, toggle **Enabled**, and point your assistant at `https://your-site.example/mcp`.
